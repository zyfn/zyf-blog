Open with the real problem, tension, or decision the reader needs to understand.

## First main section

Build the smallest useful model before adding implementation details.

<Callout title="Boundary">
State an important limitation, condition, or distinction here.
</Callout>

## Evidence or mechanism

Use code, a table, or a figure only when it materially improves understanding.

## Adoption boundary

End with what the reader can now judge or do, including the conditions that would change the conclusion.
